# Attribution follows the evidence

## Federal data

**Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center.** (2020). *Landsat 8–9 Operational Land Imager / Thermal Infrared Sensor Level-2, Collection 2* [dataset]. U.S. Geological Survey. `DOI:10.5066/P9OGBGM6`. Starter scene: `LC09_L2SP_042035_20240830_02_T1`; acquired 30 August 2024. Credit: USGS EROS and NASA Landsat program. Distributed via Microsoft Planetary Computer's STAC and cloud-optimized raster services. The [USGS product page](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-level-2-science-products) states no use restrictions; [USGS confirms cloud-hosted Landsat data remain public domain](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/are-landsat-data-cloud-still-considered-be-within-public-domain).

**U.S. Census Bureau.** (2020). *2020 Census TIGERweb, Census Tracts layer 6; POP100 total population*. [Service record](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Census2020/tigerWMS_Census2020/MapServer/6). Actual geometry and population retrieved 7 November 2026. The [Census TIGER public-domain statement](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/archives/2014-pr/cb14-208.html) supports redistribution. Exact query parameters, source dates, transformations and hashes are retained with the data. Counts refer to whole tracts unless a labeled estimate is explicitly computed.

## Infrastructure

Jupyter Book 2 (Jupyter Book contributors, BSD-3-Clause) uses MyST (MyST contributors, MIT). JupyterLite (JupyterLite contributors, BSD-3-Clause) and its Pyodide kernel (BSD-3-Clause) provide browser Python. Pyodide uses MPL-2.0 and includes separately licensed packages. GeoTIFF.js and proj4js are licensed open-source infrastructure for real COG reads and coordinate transformations; their bundled dependencies retain their own terms. The distribution includes full collected software license files under `notices/`, including the vendored browser libraries and transitive notices.

No repository-wide license has been selected for original prose, code or artwork. Third-party software and federal datasets keep their own terms. Citation is not agency endorsement or a replacement for redistribution notices.
